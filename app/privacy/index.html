Privacy policy

Last updated October 30, 2026

Lead Desk gives contractors a website form and a dashboard to follow up on the requests it collects. This page explains what we store and why. "You" means the contractor with an account. "Visitors" means the people who fill in a contractor's form.

What we store

  • Your account: your business name, email address, and a scrambled (hashed) version of your password. We cannot read your password.
  • Your form settings: the wording, colors, services, phone number, alert address and allowed websites you choose.
  • Leads: what visitors type into your form (name, phone, email, city, service, project details), the page they sent it from, any ad tags in that page address, and the notes, values and statuses you add.
  • Abuse protection: a one-way fingerprint of the visitor's network address, kept for about two hours to limit spam. We do not store the address itself with the lead.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share leads, use them for advertising, or contact your customers.
  • We do not put tracking or advertising scripts on your website or in the form.
  • We use one cookie, only on Lead Desk itself, to keep you signed in. It is not used for tracking.

Who processes the data

Lead Desk runs on Netlify, which hosts the site and stores the data. If you add an alert address, each new lead is sent to that service (for example Slack, Discord or Zapier) under their terms.

Your choices

  • Export all your leads at any time from Settings.
  • Delete a single lead for good, for example when a customer asks you to.
  • Delete your account from Settings. This removes your account, your form and all of your leads.

For the people who fill in a form

The business whose form you used decides what happens with your request. Contact them to see, change or delete your details. They can delete them from Lead Desk in one step.

Your own privacy policy

When you collect customer details on your website, your website needs its own privacy policy. You can say that you use Lead Desk to receive estimate requests.

Contact
