What it is, and what it is not

Lead Desk is

  • A free website form and a lead list for one business
  • Open source. Anyone can read the code on GitHub
  • Private. Each account sees only its own leads

Lead Desk is not

  • A lead seller. Your leads are never shared or sold
  • An auto-texter. It never messages your customers
  • A big CRM with team seats, invoices or scheduling

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no paid plan and no card on file. The code is open source, so it will stay available even if this hosted version ever changes.

Who can see my leads?

Only you, when you sign in. Leads are stored with the account they belong to and are never shared, sold or used for ads. Read the privacy policy.

Will it slow down my website?

No. The form is one small file that loads after the rest of your page, with no tracking scripts.

Does it work with my site builder?

If your builder lets you add an HTML or embed block, it works. The dashboard has step by step instructions for WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Webflow and Shopify.

How do I get told about a new lead?

The dashboard shows new leads with a count in the menu. For a phone alert, connect Slack or Discord, or use Zapier to send yourself a text or email.

Can I take my data with me?

Yes. Export every lead to a CSV file that opens in Excel or Google Sheets. When you delete your account, your leads are deleted too.
