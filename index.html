    How it works

    1. Put your own copy online

      One click installs Lead Desk on your free Netlify account. You choose the dashboard password. Your leads are stored in your account, not ours.

    2. Make your form

      Pick your trade, business name and color. You get a short piece of code.

    3. Paste it on your website

      Works on WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Webflow and plain HTML. New leads show up at your own address, ending in /admin.

    Set it up in about 15 minutes

    You do not need to write code. You do need a free Netlify account and a free GitHub account.

    Step 1. Put your copy online

    Netlify will copy Lead Desk into your own GitHub and publish it.

    Deploy to Netlify
    • Connect GitHub when Netlify asks.
    • Fill in ADMIN_PASSWORD (12 or more characters) and BUSINESS_NAME.
    • When it finishes, copy your site address. It looks like amber-fox-1234.netlify.app.

    Step 3. Paste it on your website
    
        
    Paste the code where you want the form to appear.
    
        
    WordPress
    1. Open the page in the editor.
    2. Add a block named Custom HTML.
    3. Paste the code and update the page.
    
        
    Wix
    1. In the editor choose Add, then Embed Code, then Embed HTML.
    2. Choose Code and paste it in.
    3. Stretch the box tall enough for the form, about 700 pixels.
    
        
    Squarespace
    1. Edit the page and add a Code block.
    2. Turn off Display Source, then paste the code.
    
        
    Webflow
    1. Drag an Embed element onto the page.
    2. Paste the code and publish.
    
        
    Plain HTML
    Paste the code into the page where the form should show.
    
        
    Some site builders block pasted code on their cheapest plans. If yours does, ask their support to allow it, or link your button to a page that does.
    
        
    Then test it. Send yourself a lead from your live site, then sign in at your-address/admin. It should be at the top of the list.
    
      
    

      
    
        Optional: get an alert on your phone for each new lead
        
    Make a Slack or Discord incoming webhook (or a Zapier or Make webhook that sends you a text or email). In Netlify open Site configuration, then Environment variables, add NOTIFY_WEBHOOK_URL with that address, and deploy again.
    
      
    
      
    
        Optional: only allow your own website to send leads
        
    Add an environment variable named ALLOWED_ORIGINS with your site address, like https://www.yourcompany.com. Separate several with commas. Deploy again.

    Questions
    
      
    Is it really free?
    Lead Desk is free and open source under the MIT license. It runs on your own Netlify account, which has a free plan that is enough for a small contractor. Netlify can change its plans, so read what they offer before you rely on it.
    
      
    Where are my customers' details kept?
    In your Netlify account, in storage only your site can reach. This project has no server of its own and never receives your leads.
    
      
    Can I change the questions on the form?
    You can change the heading, button, color and services here. To add or remove fields you edit leaddesk.js in your copy of the project. It is one short file.
    
      
    What if I forget the dashboard password?
    Open your site in Netlify, go to Environment variables, set a new ADMIN_PASSWORD and deploy again. Your leads stay where they are.
